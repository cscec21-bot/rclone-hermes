rclone-hermes

这是一个仅供所有者本人使用的 rclone 客户端：把所有者自己电脑上的笔记，单向复制到所有者自己的 Google 云端硬盘文件夹。它不对其他用户提供服务。

A personal rclone client used only by its owner to copy the owner's own notes from the owner's own computer to the owner's own Google Drive folder. It is not offered to anyone else.
